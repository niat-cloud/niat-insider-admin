The Media Club’s Color Collage Competition offered a way to look more closely at everyday surroundings. In the collage described here, sky, campus architecture, greenery and sunlight come together in a quiet visual story.

Blue Skies and Warm Light

The dominant blues suggest peace, openness and imagination to me. White clouds and golden sunlight add warmth. Some frames contain dramatic skies; others focus on brighter daylight or calmer landscapes.

Those differences give each photograph its own mood while allowing the images to sit together as a composition.

Nature Alongside Campus Architecture

Clouds spreading across the sky and sunlight reaching through trees draw attention to natural patterns. Campus buildings and other structures appear alongside them, bringing human-made forms into the same view.

The interest lies partly in observation: noticing light and colour in scenes that might otherwise pass without a second look.

Telling a Story Across Several Frames

The collage does not depend on a single subject. Its arrangement connects different perspectives, creating a sense of continuity and rhythm. I read the overall mood as one of tranquillity, freedom and inspiration.

What the Competition Encouraged

The event encouraged participants to experiment with composition and communicate through images. This collage captures that idea for me: familiar surroundings can become expressive when someone pays attention to how colours, light and subjects work together.
