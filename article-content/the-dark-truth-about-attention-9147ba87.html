Many people want attention. Fewer stop to ask why they are receiving it.

Attention can follow talent or success, but it can also follow controversy. Being noticed does not automatically mean creating something valuable.

Keeping Growth in View

Chasing recognition can pull attention away from the work or values that mattered in the first place. This reflection argues for focusing on growth, useful work and excellence.

Recognition With Meaning

There is no need to make attention the measure of every effort. What the work contributes—and what you learn while doing it—can matter more than how widely it is noticed.
