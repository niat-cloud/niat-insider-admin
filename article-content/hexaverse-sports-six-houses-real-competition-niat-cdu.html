Introduction

The Hexaverse Sports Event happened on 12 and 13 March at NIAT Chaitanya Deemed to be University.

Two full days. Six house groups. The kind of event where everyone is either competing or screaming from the sidelines — there is no in-between.

How It Was Organised: Six Houses

The college divided us into six houses:

  • Himadri
  • Agni
  • Prithvi
  • Samudhra
  • Vajra
  • Vayu

Each house got its own identity, and students were genuinely proud to represent theirs.

The college management also brought in bouncers and security staff — not to be intimidating, but to keep things safe and organised once the competition got intense. Which it did.

Day One: On Campus

Thursday started on the college grounds with basketball and badminton.

The energy was immediate. People were playing like they actually cared, like their house's reputation was on the line. The spectators were loud. The courts were packed. Nobody was half-hearted about any of it.

Day Two: Off Campus at PMR Cricket County

Friday moved the event to PMR Cricket County, away from campus.

That shift changed the feel of it — more space, more room for bigger games. Cricket came first, then kabaddi, volleyball, throwball, athletics, kho-kho and more. By 5 p.m. everything was done.

And the energy never dipped. Not in the heat, not by day two. People showed up for their house.

The Results

Agni took first place. Himadri came second.

The trophy ceremony happened after all the competitions wrapped up. And then — this is the part that made it feel less like a tournament and more like an actual college memory — there was music, dancing, celebration. Everyone together, not just the winning house.

Conclusion

Hexaverse was worth the two days.

It was the kind of event that genuinely brought people together, not just for the competition but for the experience of being on the same side as your house. That matters more than the trophy does.
