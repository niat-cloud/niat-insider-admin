Introduction

In my first 30 days at NIAT Vijayawada, I did something I had never done before: I stood for an election.

Along with academics and technical activities, NIAT gives equal importance to creativity and self-expression, and the Arts Club is where that happens. I joined it as an ordinary member. Within a month I was its Vice President. This is how that went, including the part where I was too nervous to speak.

Finding a Creative Space on Campus

The Arts Club gives students room to explore interests beyond studies — art, design, performances, creative ideas.

What it really does is create a balance. Technical work fills most of the week, and the club is what stops campus life from becoming only that. It made the whole thing more engaging for me, and that was before I had any role in it.

Standing in the Club Elections

I started by simply taking part in Arts Club activities and exploring what was on offer. Then I decided to go a step further and contest the club elections.

This was completely new to me. I had to campaign. I had to speak to people I barely knew. I had to share my ideas and present myself with confidence I did not entirely feel.

I was nervous at the start. I treated it as a challenge instead of a reason to back out, and I gave it everything I had.

I was elected Vice President of the Arts Club — which became one of my proudest achievements, and it happened inside a month.

What the Vice President Actually Does

A leadership role comes with real responsibilities. As Vice President I am involved in:

  • planning club activities
  • coordinating with team members
  • making sure events run smoothly on the day

The club is structured so students take genuine ownership and work as a team. That is where the leadership qualities, communication skills and decision-making come from — not from the title, but from having to actually do these things. Every activity turns into a learning experience.

A Place Where Everyone Gets a Voice

The Arts Club is not only about activities. It is about giving students a voice.

Everyone is encouraged to share ideas, suggest activities and take part without fear of being judged. That open, supportive environment is what lets students express themselves freely — and for a lot of people, including me, it is where confidence starts.

How Creativity Changed Me

Being part of the Arts Club has helped me grow in ways I did not expect.

My confidence improved. My creativity improved. My ability to talk to people — the thing that terrified me during the campaign — improved most of all.

It also taught me that learning is not limited to academics. Creativity and expression matter just as much for becoming a whole person.

Conclusion

Looking back, my journey in the Arts Club has been genuinely meaningful. From turning up at activities to holding a leadership role, every step added something.

In 30 days I did not only explore my creative side — I found confidence and leadership skills I did not know I had. The Arts Club at NIAT is not just a club. It is a platform that helps students find out what they are capable of.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What is the Arts Club at NIAT Vijayawada?

A student club for creative interests beyond academics — art, design, performances and creative ideas — which gives campus life a balance alongside technical work.

Q2. How are Arts Club leaders chosen?

By club elections. Candidates campaign, speak to students and present their ideas before members vote.

Q3. Can a first-year student hold a leadership position?

Yes. This student contested and was elected Vice President within her first 30 days on campus.

Q4. What does the Arts Club Vice President do?

Plans activities, coordinates with team members and makes sure events run smoothly.

Q5. Do you need artistic talent to join?

No. The article describes it as open to anyone wanting to share ideas or take part, with no fear of judgement.
