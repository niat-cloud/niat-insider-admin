Introduction

"There's always that moment before something big in college where you don't really know what's coming."

That is where I was before my first fest at NIAT x NRI. I am a first-year B.Tech student and I assumed a fest was something you attended — you stand at the side, you watch, you clap, you go home. It took one day to find out that is not how it works here.

The Moment I Stopped Watching and Started Taking Part

The change came when I actually joined in — dance, sports, club activities.

The difference between watching a fest and being in one is not small. It is the difference between remembering an event and remembering a day. Once you are in it, you are not an audience member any more, and the whole thing becomes yours.

What Fest Days Actually Feel Like

Fest days are movement, energy and constant activity.

You are going from one performance to the next activity with your friends, all day, and there is no quiet stretch in the middle. It is tiring in the specific way that only a good day is tiring.

From Participant to Performer

Here is the thing I would tell anyone hesitating: college events are not about perfection, they are about involvement.

Nobody is grading your dance. Nobody is comparing your sport to anyone else's. The only real mistake is deciding you are not good enough to take part, which is the one decision that guarantees you miss the day entirely.

The Reality: Not Perfect, But Memorable

I will be honest — the scheduling was not flawless.

Things ran late, plans shifted, not everything happened exactly when it was supposed to. And none of that is what I remember. What stays with you is not the schedule — it is the experience.

The People Make the Day

The same activity with different people is a different day.

Sharing it with friends is what turns an ordinary event into something you will bring up years later. That part is not organised by anybody; it just happens, and it is most of the value.

Conclusion

What I realised at NIAT x NRI is that college is made of moments where you try something new and step forward despite the hesitation.

College fests are not just events you watch. They are experiences you create.
