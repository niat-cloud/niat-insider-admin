During my first year at NIAT, I realized that effort is only one part of learning. The people around me—and how comfortable I felt asking questions or making mistakes—mattered too.

Encouragement Made Participation Easier

A word of support could make it easier to try something unfamiliar. Feeling encouraged helped me see questions and uncertainty as part of learning rather than reasons to stay quiet.

Curiosity mattered in that environment. A simple question could lead to a discussion, a project idea or a new interest.

Learning Together in the Gen AI Club

The Gen AI Club brought students together to discuss artificial intelligence, technology and innovation. What stood out to me was the opportunity to learn from one another.

Sharing knowledge, helping with difficulties and respecting different ideas made participation feel more collaborative. The atmosphere gave creativity and experimentation room to develop.

A Different Response to Failure

The BRAVE Competition reinforced this lesson. In the experience described here, nearly 15 unsuccessful business negotiations came before a successful 16th attempt.

Those setbacks could have been a reason to stop. Instead, they became opportunities to improve communication, negotiation and resilience. I connected that experience with the value of an environment where mistakes can be discussed and learned from.

Support in Everyday Interactions

I noticed the importance of small actions: listening to an idea, offering encouragement or helping someone keep going. Trust and friendships grew through those interactions.

Feeling that a contribution would be respected made it easier to speak up, take responsibility and consider unfamiliar ideas. Support did not remove the work; it helped make trying feel worthwhile.

What I Value About the Environment

Facilities and programmes matter, but so do the people involved in everyday learning. When I look back, I remember encouragement, shared questions and the confidence that developed gradually. For me, those experiences explain why a positive learning environment is such an important part of college.
