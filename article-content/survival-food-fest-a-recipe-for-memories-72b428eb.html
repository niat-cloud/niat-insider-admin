At NIAT × NSRIT, the Survival & Food Fest brings food stalls, teamwork and friendly competition into the same event. The activity starts before the campus fills with visitors: teams need ideas, menus, themes and a plan for running their stalls.

Planning a Stall Together

Students brainstorm themes, prepare menus and think about how to attract visitors. Presentation matters alongside the food. Each stall gives a team a chance to turn an idea into something other students can experience.

When the Campus Becomes a Marketplace

On the day, colourful stalls, food aromas and busy crowds change the atmosphere. Students move between spicy snacks, drinks and homemade specialities, trying different dishes and supporting friends.

The bustle makes the event social as well as competitive. Behind each stall is a group sharing responsibilities and trying to keep things moving.

What the “Survival” Challenge Adds

Teams need to manage resources, respond to unexpected problems and make decisions under pressure. That creates opportunities to practise communication, leadership and the practical side of running a small venture.

Working together matters as much as having an idea. Planning, creativity and perseverance become visible in the way a team handles the day.

What Remains After the Stalls Close

The event’s memories include the preparation, laughter and shared effort as well as the food. In this account, the Survival & Food Fest is valuable because it gives students a setting to learn through responsibility while enjoying campus life together.
