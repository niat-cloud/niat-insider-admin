Compliments are easy to enjoy. Honest correction can be harder to hear, especially when it concerns a project, presentation or decision you care about.

The Feedback That Helps You Improve

A friend who notices a mistake may tell you something uncomfortable. Later, you might recognize that their honesty helped you see what needed work.

Correction is useful when it comes with care and an intention to help.

Appreciating More Than Praise

Encouragement matters, and so does feedback you can learn from. A friendship can make room for both: someone who celebrates your effort and is honest when there is a way to improve.

Why can a friend’s correction matter?
