Introduction

The NIAT Jaipur freshers party ran for six hours and did not drop in energy once.

It kicked off at 12 p.m. and continued till 6 p.m., and it was the first time most of our batch had been in one room together with nothing to do but enjoy it.

What Was on the Programme

The event packed several different kinds of performance into the afternoon:

  • Band performances
  • Dance acts
  • A DJ
  • A ramp walk

Having that range is what kept the energy up across six hours. There was always something different starting as the last thing finished.

The People Who Made It Happen

The management handled venue logistics, and around 20 NSGC volunteers coordinated operations on the day.

That is the part an audience never notices. Six hours of continuous programming does not run itself, and a team of twenty students kept it moving.

Nobody Was Left Out

Transportation was arranged for students, so everyone could attend and enjoy the event without any hassle.

It sounds like an administrative footnote. In practice it is the reason participation was as broad as it was — plenty of students who live off campus would otherwise have missed it entirely.

Conclusion

The freshers party did not just welcome new students warmly. It created lasting memories filled with fun, laughter and togetherness.

For most of us it was the first day the batch felt like a batch.
