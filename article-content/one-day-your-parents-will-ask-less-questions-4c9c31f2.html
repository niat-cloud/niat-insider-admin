As students, we sometimes joke about how many questions our parents ask. Did you eat? How are your studies? When are you coming home? The questions can feel endless.

The Care Behind Familiar Questions

Imagine those conversations becoming less frequent as time changes things. Questions that once seemed repetitive might become the ones we miss.

It can be easy to overlook care when it is part of an ordinary day.

Appreciating the Conversations We Have

This is a reminder to value the people who check on us. A familiar question can be worth more than it seems in the moment.

One day, the questions we ignore may become the conversations we wish we could hear again.
