Introduction

Our Gen AI teacher at NIAT Chaitanya recently introduced us to personalised and AI-driven learning — a topic that is reshaping education as we know it.

The session opened my eyes to how modern technology is turning traditional teaching into something smarter and more effective. Here is what we covered.

The End of One-Size-Fits-All

Our teacher explained that the old "one-size-fits-all" approach to education is gradually being replaced by adaptive learning systems.

These systems adjust both the content and the pace based on each student's performance. That means every student can learn at their own ability level, which makes it easier to understand a concept properly rather than being carried along at somebody else's speed.

AI Tutors and Assistants

The teacher also talked about AI-powered tutors and assistants, using two real examples:

  • Squirrel AI
  • Kyron Learning

These platforms support students at any time — helping with the syllabus, clearing doubts, and simplifying difficult topics. The significant part is that learning stops being limited to the hours you are physically in a classroom.

Predictive Analytics: Spotting Problems Early

This was the part I had not thought about before.

Our teacher explained how colleges and universities use AI to analyse student data and identify students who might be struggling. That lets teachers act early and provide support before a problem becomes a failed exam, rather than afterwards.

Intelligent Textbooks

We were also introduced to intelligent textbooks.

Unlike a regular book, these are digital and interactive, with instant feedback, personalised content and better engagement. It is a straightforward idea that makes learning noticeably more interesting.

Conclusion

Overall the session was genuinely informative, and it changed how I think about the subject.

It helped us understand that artificial intelligence is not just a technology to learn about. It is a tool that can improve education itself and help students actually reach their goals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What is adaptive learning?

A system that adjusts the content and pace of learning to each student's performance, so students can learn at their own ability level.

Q2. What AI tutoring tools were discussed?

Squirrel AI and Kyron Learning — platforms that support students outside class hours by helping with the syllabus, clearing doubts and simplifying difficult topics.

Q3. How do colleges use predictive analytics?

By analysing student data to identify students likely to be struggling, so teachers can step in early with support.

Q4. What is an intelligent textbook?

A digital, interactive textbook offering instant feedback and personalised content, rather than fixed printed material.
