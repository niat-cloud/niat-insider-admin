Introduction

NIAT Hyderabad has brought in a policy update that has everyone talking.

The management has introduced strict attendance rules that directly affect exam eligibility. Attendance is no longer just about showing up — it now decides whether you can write your semester exams at all. Here is exactly what the thresholds are.

The Three Attendance Thresholds

The rule works in three bands:

  • 60% attendance or above — eligible to write semester exams
  • Less than 60% — not allowed to appear for one exam
  • Below 40% — not eligible to write two exams

That makes it clear how seriously the college is treating attendance and academic discipline. The gap between 59% and 60% is now the gap between sitting an exam and not sitting it.

How the Percentage Is Actually Calculated

This part matters, and it works in students' favour.

The attendance percentage is calculated from your NIAT app records, with an additional 20% added as extra support.

That extra percentage exists because students miss classes for legitimate reasons — exams, events and other academic activities where marking attendance is not always possible. So the figure you see is not purely raw class attendance.

Success Coaches Are Involved

The policy is not only administrative. Success coaches are actively monitoring attendance.

They are contacting the parents of students whose attendance falls below 60%, so that both the student and the family know where things stand and can do something about it before an exam is at stake.

Whether you find that supportive or uncomfortable probably depends on your attendance.

Why the Policy Exists

The rules may seem strict, and they are.

The stated purpose is to encourage regular attendance, better learning habits and improved academic performance. It is a reminder to stay consistent and take responsibility for your own studies rather than discovering the problem in exam week.

Conclusion

Maintaining good attendance is now more important than it has ever been here.

The thresholds are clear, the calculation includes a 20% allowance, and your parents will hear about it before you fall below the line. Follow the rules, stay consistent, and none of it will ever affect you.
