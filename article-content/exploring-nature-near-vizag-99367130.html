When I first thought of Visakhapatnam, I pictured its coastline. Exploring nature near Vizag showed me another side of the region: hills, forests, valleys and quieter landscapes beyond the beaches.

Leaving the Busy Routine Behind

Moving away from the city centre, I noticed roads giving way to greenery and traffic becoming less noticeable. After the schedules, deadlines and activity of college life, that change felt refreshing.

Exploration did not always have to mean travelling far. I began appreciating the natural surroundings closer to where I was studying instead of thinking only about distant destinations.

Noticing More by Slowing Down

Outdoors, I paid more attention to the shapes of hills, patterns of leaves, movement of clouds and sounds of birds. A walk, a viewpoint or a quiet moment in greenery could be memorable without a complicated plan.

Different landscapes offered different experiences. A forest felt different from a hilltop, just as a valley felt different from the coast. That variety made me more curious about wildlife, geology, ecosystems and conservation.

A Change of Scene and Perspective

Spending time in a peaceful setting gave me room to reflect. Concerns that felt overwhelming indoors sometimes seemed easier to think through after stepping away from routine.

The BRAVE Competition later reinforced a related idea for me: a new environment can create a new perspective. I connected that lesson with the space I found while exploring outdoors.

What Exploring Nature Gave Me

I may not remember every trail or viewpoint, but I will remember the feeling of leaving a busy day behind. Exploring helped me appreciate that Vizag’s natural identity extends beyond its coastline. For me, the experience began with making time to step outside and look more closely.
