One of my favourite evening experiences in Vizag has been watching the sky change from Kailasagiri. The hilltop view brings the city and coastline into the same scene, and it gave me a welcome pause from classes, assignments and projects.

Watching the Evening Arrive

During my visit, the sunlight softened and the sky shifted through orange, pink and gold. City lights began appearing below. The atmosphere felt slower, and I found myself paying attention to details I would usually miss during a busy day.

I noticed other visitors pause too. Conversations became quieter, and people who had been taking photographs turned their attention toward the view.

The Coastline, Sea and City Lights

From Kailasagiri, I could look along the coastline, see evening light on the Bay of Bengal and watch the city gradually light up. That combination is what stays with me.

Different evenings had different colours. Some were vivid; others were softer. The briefness of the changing light made me want to appreciate it while it lasted.

Making Time to Pause

College life can keep attention fixed on the next task. Standing above the city gave me a different sense of perspective.

The BRAVE Competition later reminded me of a related lesson: while working toward a goal, it helps to recognize how far you have come. For me, an evening view offered space for that kind of reflection.

Why This View Became a Favourite

I remember the feeling as much as the scenery: calm, reflective and present. I may forget individual evenings, but the colours, coastline and emerging city lights remain clear. That is why Kailasagiri became one of my favourite places to pause in Vizag.
