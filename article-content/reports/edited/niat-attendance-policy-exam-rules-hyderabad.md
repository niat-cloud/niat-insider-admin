**Introduction**

NIAT Hyderabad has introduced a major policy update, and it has everyone talking.

The management has brought in strict attendance rules that directly affect exam eligibility. Attendance is no longer just about showing up: it now decides whether you can write your semester exams at all. Here is how the rules work, how your percentage is calculated, and what happens if you fall behind.

---

#### H2: The Rules at a Glance

| Your attendance | What it means for semester exams |
|---|---|
| **60% or above** | Eligible to write your semester exams |
| **Below 60%** | Not allowed to appear for **one** exam |
| **Below 40%** | Not eligible to write **two** exams |

The message is clear: the college is taking attendance and academic discipline seriously, and the consequences start the moment you drop under 60%.

#### H2: How Your Attendance Is Calculated

Your attendance percentage comes from your **NIAT app records**, with an **additional 20% added** as extra support.

That extra 20% exists for a practical reason. Students sometimes miss classes because of exams, events and other academic activities, where marking attendance is not always possible. The adjustment is there to cover those situations — not to cover ordinary missed classes.

#### H2: Success Coaches Are Watching, and Parents Will Hear

The rules are not just written down and left alone.

**Success coaches** are actively monitoring student attendance. If your attendance falls **below 60%**, they contact your **parents**, so that both you and your family know where things stand and can take steps to improve it before exams arrive.

#### H2: Why the College Brought This In

The policy may seem strict, and on first reading it is.

But it was introduced to encourage regular attendance, better learning habits and improved academic performance. It is a reminder to stay consistent and responsible in your studies across the whole semester, not just in the weeks before exams.

**Conclusion**

Maintaining good attendance is now more important than ever at NIAT Hyderabad.

Stay at 60% or above and you are eligible for every semester exam. Follow the rules, stay committed, and you avoid the restrictions entirely — and give yourself the best chance of performing well when the exams come.
