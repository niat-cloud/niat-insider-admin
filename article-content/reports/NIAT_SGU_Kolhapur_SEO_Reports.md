### ⚠️ READ THIS FIRST — WHY THE BODIES ARE NOT HERE

Every section below is complete **except section 6, the recommended body.**

On the six earlier campuses, fetching a page returned its full text, so I could restructure it
faithfully. On this campus the fetch tool enforces a 125-character quote limit and now refuses
outright: *"I can't reproduce the full article, but I can summarize it or pull a short quote."*

I have each article's **headings verbatim**, its opening and closing sentence, and every named
place, number, event and person. I do **not** have the paragraph text. Writing bodies from that
would mean reconstructing prose I have not read, which is how the four errors your pipeline caught
were produced — a dropped competition list, an invented "at midnight", an invented "three hours",
and "lining on the badminton court" generalised to "the courts".

**So section 6 of each report gives the body *plan*** — the H2 structure anchored to the article's
real existing headings, plus the specific facts that must survive the rewrite. Hand me the body
text (an export from your admin tooling is fastest and exact) and I will write the prose into these
plans.

**Everything else is ready to apply now,** including fixes to five broken titles and H1s.

|---|---|
