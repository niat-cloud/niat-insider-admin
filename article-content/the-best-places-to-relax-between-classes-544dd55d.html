When assignments, presentations and deadlines crowd the day at NIAT × NSRIT, I appreciate having somewhere to pause. A few ordinary campus spaces became the places where my friends and I could talk, laugh and make a stressful day feel lighter.

Tree Benches and Familiar Company

For me, the best relaxing spot inside the campus is definitely the benches under the trees. After long classes and continuous discussions, sitting there feels calm and refreshing. Most students gather there while waiting for buses or during free time between classes.

That place honestly became more than just a sitting area for our batch. It slowly turned into one of the main student hangout spots where conversations never really end. Sometimes we discuss projects, future plans, and technology. Other times we simply joke around and laugh about random classroom moments.

My friends — Surya, Azam, Sawith Varma, Monica, and Vishali — and I spent many evenings there together. Even stressful situations somehow feel lighter while sitting there with friends and talking freely.

The Waiting Room Near the Washrooms

Another place students often relax in is the waiting room near the washrooms. Even though it sounds like an ordinary place, it somehow became another unofficial student corner during breaks. People sit there casually discussing assignments, presentations, faculty, and everyday college life.

Cafeteria Breaks

The cafeteria is another important relaxing spot inside campus. No matter how stressful the day becomes, food breaks somehow improve everyone’s mood. Some students go there mainly for snacks, while others go just to escape classroom stress for a while.

One thing I noticed at NIAT is that students rarely sit quietly during breaks. With our group’s energy and humour, even small free periods quickly become full conversations and laughter.

The funniest moments usually happen during stress discussions. Students begin by seriously talking about deadlines and workload, but within minutes somebody makes a random joke and suddenly the entire group starts laughing instead of stressing.

When a Faculty Member Joined the Conversation

One memorable moment happened when we were sitting near the tree benches discussing how stressful college life was becoming. Unexpectedly, one of our faculty members joined the conversation with us. At first everyone became silent and awkward, but later the conversation became surprisingly comfortable and funny.

That moment honestly made the campus environment feel more natural and friendly.

Why Those Small Breaks Matter

Looking back now, I feel relaxing spots inside a campus are important not because of the place itself, but because of the people and memories connected to them. Sometimes the best part of college life is not the classroom or events — it is those small moments spent with friends between stressful classes.

And honestly, those small breaks are what make busy college life feel enjoyable and memorable.
