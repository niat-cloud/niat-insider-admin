The Friend Who Remains Familiar

This is the friend who still laughs at the same jokes, treats people with respect and does not let achievement replace their values.

What Humility Preserves

Achievements may change a lifestyle without needing to change someone’s character. The reflection is about that kind of humility: continuing to treat people well while moving forward.
