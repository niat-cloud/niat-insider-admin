Introduction

Our college introduced QR codes in classrooms to collect student feedback, and it is actually working.

The management reviewed our responses and tackled the biggest issue first: sports facilities that badly needed attention. Here is what has changed since.

What the Feedback Showed

The institution examined the feedback submissions and prioritised the biggest student concerns.

Sports facilities came out as the most significant area needing improvement. Many students raised concerns about the lack of proper equipment and infrastructure — and because the feedback was collected systematically, the scale of the concern was visible rather than anecdotal.

The Basketball Rings Are In

Renovation work has started, and the first result is done.

The most important single issue was the absence of basketball rings — and their installation has now been successfully completed.

That is the detail worth noting. A feedback system that produces a visible change is a feedback system students will keep using.

What Students Have Asked for Next

Students have requested essential sports equipment including:

  • Tennis balls
  • Basketballs
  • Volleyballs
  • and more

The management has acknowledged these requests and is expected to provide them soon.

The Courts Are Next

On the courts themselves, the management has given an assurance that this work will also be completed shortly, which should improve playing conditions.

Conclusion

The introduction of QR codes has made it easier for students to raise a concern, and easier for the management to act on one in good time.

The basketball rings are the proof. Everything else on the list is now a test of whether the system keeps working.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do students give feedback at Chaitanya Hyderabad?

Through QR codes placed in classrooms, which students scan and submit concerns through directly.

Q2. What did students raise as the biggest problem?

Sports facilities — specifically a lack of proper equipment and infrastructure.

Q3. What has actually changed as a result?

Basketball rings have been installed, which was the single biggest issue raised.

Q4. What else have students asked for?

Essential sports equipment including tennis balls, basketballs and volleyballs, which management has acknowledged.

Q5. What is still pending?

Work on the courts, which management has assured will be completed shortly, and the equipment requests.
