The green spaces around our NIAT NSRIT campus in Sontyam are a welcome change after an eight-hour coding stretch. Looking out at lawns and the mountain backdrop feels very different from staring at a stubborn UI/UX layout problem.

A Quiet Patch After a Difficult Peer Review

The academic blocks sit alongside garden patches and outdoor seating. After an intense peer-review session left my creative confidence a little bruised, I took my notebook to a shaded grassy patch near the boundary wall.

I wanted fifteen quiet minutes with trees instead of a screen. For me, that short break was a chance to reset.

The Sprinkler Had Other Plans

You know that peaceful moment when you lie on the grass to look at the clouds? I managed to choose a spot directly in front of a hidden automated sprinkler.

It switched on with a hiss. My exit was considerably less graceful than my arrival.

Why I Still Value the Green Spaces

Having outdoor space close to the classrooms makes it easier for me to step away from work for a moment. The break does not need to be elaborate—even if this one became unexpectedly eventful.

I would check for damp soil before putting a college bag down, though. An abstract mud stain across your paperwork is not the souvenir you want for an evening lab.

See you on the lawns—or catching me wiping grass clippings off my laptop.
