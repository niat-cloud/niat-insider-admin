Introduction

There are three hostels on the VGU campus, and for NIAT students the choice is effectively made already.

Narayana Hostel is newly built and houses almost 99% of NIAT students — and once you have seen it, the reason is fairly obvious. Here is what living there is actually like.

The Rooms

Narayana offers a modern and comfortable set-up. The rooms are clean and well maintained, and they come with:

  • Air conditioning — which makes them workable in every season, and in Jaipur that is not a small thing
  • An attached bathroom, including a geyser
  • Modern lighting and interiors, which keep the rooms feeling fresh rather than institutional

What Else Is Inside the Hostel

Two features stand out beyond the rooms themselves:

  • A badminton court inside the hostel, which adds a genuine recreation option without leaving the building
  • Water coolers on every floor, so drinking water is always within reach

In Jaipur's summer, the second one matters more than it sounds.

The Advantages of Living on Campus

Being inside the campus gives you easy access to:

  • the cricket ground
  • the volleyball court
  • the library

That makes balancing academics and everything else considerably simpler, because nothing requires a journey first.

Safety is the other real advantage — the campus environment is secure and well managed, which matters to students and more still to their families.

Five Minutes From Class

The hostel is a 5-minute walk from the NIAT department.

That sounds minor until you compare it with the alternative. Article 1 on this site describes the walk from the gate to the 4th floor of the NIAT block as a small hike; students in flats are travelling around 2 km each way. Five minutes saves time every single day, and it is the difference between making a 9 a.m. class comfortably and not.

The Food, Honestly

Like most hostels, the mess food can be average at times. It is manageable and it is consistent, which is about the fairest thing anyone says about mess food anywhere.

What actually makes hostel life worth it is not the menu. It is the experience — the friendships, the late-night conversations, the memories that come out of living alongside the same people.

Conclusion

If you want to get the most out of campus life, build real friendships, and live somewhere convenient and secure, the hostel is a good decision.

It is not just a place to stay. It becomes a core part of the whole college experience.
