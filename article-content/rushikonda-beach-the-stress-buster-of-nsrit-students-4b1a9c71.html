Assignments, records, internals, labs—and the familiar “Bro, did you complete the submission?” College can leave students ready for a change of scene. For me, Rushikonda Beach is part of that lighter side of NSRIT student life.

Photos, Games and Time by the Water

The sand, sea breeze and beach-road views create a very different atmosphere from a day of submissions. Students become photographers and reel creators, sometimes taking enough pictures to fill a gallery.

Some play cricket or football on the sand. Others sit and talk. The changing colours of the evening sky add another reason to pause.

The Journey Has Its Own Stories

The outing can become memorable before anyone reaches the beach. Friends sing, make jokes and turn the beach-road stretch into their own travel vlog. Someone usually announces, “Bro, this feels like Goa!”

Snacks and the Student Budget

Corn, ice creams, spicy snacks and cool drinks become part of the trip. Plans to save money can quickly change once snacks and extra auto rides enter the picture.

I prefer the evening atmosphere, when the day feels cooler. Mornings can be peaceful too; the afternoon heat is less appealing.

A Break I Associate With Friends

Rushikonda is memorable to me because of the company, jokes and time away from assignments. A weekend outing or a small celebration does not need to be elaborate to become part of the college story.
