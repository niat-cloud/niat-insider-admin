College life is full of assignments, records, internals, labs, and the legendary question: “Bro, did you complete the submission?” After dealing with all this stress, every student needs one perfect escape spot. For students of NSRIT, that perfect place is definitely Rushikonda Beach.

Located in the beautiful city of Visakhapatnam, Rushikonda Beach is one of the most attractive and peaceful places to visit with friends. The blue water, soft sand, cool breeze, and scenic beach roads make the place look straight out of a movie. It is the kind of place where students suddenly become photographers, poets, and reel creators all at once.

The beach is famous for its natural beauty and relaxing atmosphere. During sunset, the sky turns orange and pink, creating an amazing view. Some students go there to play cricket or football on the sand, while others simply sit near the waves and enjoy the peaceful vibe. Of course, no trip is complete without taking hundreds of photos that will probably never be deleted from the gallery.

One of the funniest parts of the trip is the journey itself. Students from NSRIT can easily reach Rushikonda Beach using public transport. First, they need to board the 700 bus from the college side and travel to Simhachalam. From Simhachalam, they can take buses like 900K or 999 towards Rushikonda Beach. The moment the bus enters Vizag beach road, everyone suddenly starts acting like they are in a travel vlog. Cool breeze hits the face, friends start singing random songs loudly, and one person always says, “Bro, this feels like Goa!”

Rushikonda Beach is also famous for its street food. Hot corn, ice creams, spicy snacks, and cool drinks make the trip even better. Most students plan to save money but somehow return home with empty pockets after buying snacks and taking extra auto rides.

The best time to visit the beach is during the evening because the weather becomes cooler and the sunset view looks amazing. Morning visits are also peaceful, but afternoon visits are dangerous unless someone wants free sunlight-powered roasting.

Overall, Rushikonda Beach is more than just a tourist spot. For students, it is a stress-relief destination, a photo spot, a hangout place, and sometimes even an emotional support system after exam results. Whether it is a random weekend plan or a celebration with friends, Rushikonda Beach never disappoints. 🌴✨
