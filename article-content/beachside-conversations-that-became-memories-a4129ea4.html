Some of my favourite college memories are conversations beside the beaches of Visakhapatnam. They rarely started with an important topic. A casual question or a comment about the day would turn into a discussion that lasted much longer than we expected.

Why Conversations Felt Different by the Sea

Classrooms have schedules, and meetings have agendas. By the ocean, our conversations felt unplanned. The waves, cool breeze and relaxed surroundings made it easier to slow down and listen.

We talked about academics, future goals and life beyond college. Sometimes a few minutes became hours. Hearing what someone else was dealing with gave me a perspective I would not have found by talking only about my own day.

Friendships Built Through Ordinary Moments

I used to think memorable friendships needed major events. These conversations showed me how much can grow through simply spending time together.

Some discussions offered advice. Others provided motivation or the comfort of being understood. Nothing extraordinary had to happen for an evening to stay with me.

We found these moments after classes, before exams and during weekends. The beach became somewhere to process what was happening and talk about what might come next.

Learning to Listen

The BRAVE Competition later reinforced a lesson I associated with those conversations: communication involves listening and understanding another point of view as much as speaking.

Taking time to hear someone’s story revealed experiences and challenges I had not expected. That made our friendships feel deeper, and it reminded me that learning also happens through people.

What I Will Remember

I may forget the exact topics we discussed. I will remember the laughter, the sound of the waves and how comfortable it felt to sit together without a plan. Those simple conversations became part of my college story.
