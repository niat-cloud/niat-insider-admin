Introduction

Finding a good hostel is one of the most important parts of student life, especially at Chaitanya Deemed to be University.

There are many hostels around the university, but only a few genuinely provide a comfortable environment, good food and a peaceful atmosphere for studying and living. These are the ones students actually recommend.

The Best Area: Around Thoranam Restaurant

Of all the hostel areas near CDU, the Thoranam Restaurant surroundings are considered one of the best locations for students.

The area is popular because several well-maintained hostels sit close together, which makes food, transport and daily necessities easy to reach. Students tend to prefer it for the lively atmosphere and, practically, because having multiple hostel options in one area means you can compare rather than take the first room you see.

The Well-Known Hostels in This Area

Three names come up repeatedly:

  • MQ Hostel
  • Siri Hostel
  • YVR Hostel

All three are known for decent rooms, good maintenance and quality food facilities. Students choose them because they strike a balance between comfort and affordability — which is the only balance that matters when you are paying for it yourself.

Why Students Rate YVR Hostel Highest

Of all of these, YVR Hostel is considered the best choice by many students.

It is appreciated for better food quality, clean rooms and comfortable living conditions. Students staying there often describe it as having a homely atmosphere — which helps with focusing on academics while still enjoying hostel life rather than merely surviving it.

Another Option: Blue Wine Hostel

Blue Wine Hostel is another good option near CDU.

It is a popular choice because of its facilities and convenient location. If you are after a comfortable stay with proper food and accommodation, it is a reliable alternative to the Thoranam area hostels.

Conclusion

Students near CDU have several hostel choices, but the Thoranam Restaurant area stands out for its environment and the quality of the hostels in it.

Whether it is YVR, MQ, Siri or Blue Wine, you can find something that suits your preferences and your budget — and being able to compare four options in one trip is worth more than any single recommendation.
