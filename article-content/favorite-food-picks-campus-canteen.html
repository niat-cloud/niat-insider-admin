Introduction

Student life is not only classes and assignments. A good part of it happens over a snack with friends, in the ten minutes between one session and the next.

One of the things I enjoy most about NIAT is how much choice there is on campus. These are the things I keep going back to, and the ones I would tell a new student to try first.

What the Canteen Stocks

There is a wider range than most people expect:

  • snacks
  • fast food
  • bakery items
  • milkshakes
  • full meals, including biryani

That spread covers both ends of the day — a quick bite between classes, or a proper meal when you have time to sit down.

"Student life isn't just about classes and assignments—it's also about small moments shared over snacks."

My Go-To Drink: The Oreo Milkshake

Of all the drinks I have tried, the Oreo milkshake is my favourite.

It is refreshing, it tastes good, and it is exactly right for a quick break in the middle of a busy day. Whenever I want something cool, that is what I order without thinking about it.

Best Fast Food: Manchuria

For fast food, Manchuria is my top pick.

The flavour is what makes it — it is the snack I enjoy most, and it is especially good when a group of us are sitting around with time to spare.

The Bakery Counter

The bakery section has several things worth trying.

I particularly enjoyed the Cooling Cake. A lot of students will tell you the Lava Cake is the one to get, and it is easily the most recommended item on that counter.

Conclusion

Good food makes campus life genuinely better, and it is a bigger part of the day than anyone admits.

If you are new to NIAT, start with the Oreo milkshake, the Manchuria and the bakery counter. Those three have become my favourite part of the campus food experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What food is available at the NIAT Vijayawada canteen?

Snacks, fast food, bakery items, milkshakes and full meals including biryani — options for a quick bite between classes or a proper sit-down meal.

Q2. What is the most recommended item?

The Lava Cake from the bakery counter, which this student says many students recommend. Her own favourites are the Oreo milkshake and the Manchuria.

Q3. Is there a bakery on campus?

Yes, there is a bakery section. The Cooling Cake and the Lava Cake are both named as favourites.

Q4. What should a new student try first?

Her recommendation is the Oreo milkshake, the Manchuria and the bakery treats.
